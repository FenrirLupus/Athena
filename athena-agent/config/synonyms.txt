# config/synonyms.txt — words that mean the same thing *in one sense*.
# One group per line; every token on the line is a member. A word may appear in at most two
# groups (a genuine second sense). Not allowed: function words, tokens with a digit, tokens of
# 16+ characters, phrases, wildcards, or two antonyms in one group.
# See proposals/recall/design-two-check.md §3.
#
# This file is the semantic check's whole quality. Edit it and re-run:
#   scripts/recall_probe.py --pairs      the labelled table it moves
#   scripts/recall_probe.py --synonyms   group counts, and every word in two groups

# sense: to utter
say reply answer respond tell echo repeat

# sense: without variation
exactly precisely verbatim

# sense: assent
ok okay fine agreed

# sense: the volume on a device
disk space storage capacity room

# sense: what remains of a quantity; `available` bridges to the not-occupied sense of free
left remaining spare available

# sense: not occupied — free's second sense, and the reason it sits in two groups
free idle available unused

# sense: an opening, or a check that someone is there
hello hey hi greetings hear listen listening

# sense: a lane's resting state versus a working one
busy working occupied

# sense: a short span of prose
sentence line phrase

# sense: brief
short brief quick
